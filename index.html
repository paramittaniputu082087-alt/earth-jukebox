Accessible listening guide

This experience supports keyboard navigation and screen readers. Use Tab to move between controls, Space or Enter to activate buttons, and the arrow keys to explore years. Select “Read current data aloud” to hear the year, temperature anomaly and mapped pitch spoken by your device. A screen reader can also read the same information on this page.

What the sounds mean: Piano notes represent the mapped data pitch; the soft ocean-like sound is digitally generated ambience; the gentle pulse is a generated rhythm. These are not recordings of real ocean sounds.

How to listen: All three layers are switched on by default. You can turn any layer off to hear the others separately. The piano notes use the supplied frequency mapping; the ocean atmosphere and rhythm are generated sounds, not recordings of the ocean.
